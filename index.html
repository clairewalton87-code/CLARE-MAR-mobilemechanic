6. Cancellation rights for distance and off-premises contracts

If you are a consumer and the contract is concluded by phone, email, online or away from our business premises, you will normally have a 14-day cancellation period for a service contract, subject to legal exceptions. If you ask us to start work during that 14-day period, we will obtain your express request to start early and your acknowledgement that your cancellation right ends once the service is fully performed. For off-premises contracts, the early-start request must be made on a durable medium, such as email or a signed form. If you then cancel after work has started, you may have to pay a proportionate amount for work properly carried out up to cancellation. If the service is fully performed during the cancellation period after the required request and acknowledgement, your cancellation right may end. Special rules can also apply to urgent repairs or maintenance that you specifically request. The service cancellation period normally ends 14 days after the day the contract is agreed.

To cancel, contact us clearly by phone or email. You can also use the cancellation form below. Cancellation rights do not affect your rights if work is faulty or not carried out with reasonable care and skill.

Before work starts, we will confirm the agreed work, price or pricing basis, appointment and applicable terms by email or on paper so you can keep them. The website enquiry checkbox does not authorise an early start or waive cancellation rights.

If you want work to begin during the cancellation period, we will ask you to confirm: “I expressly request CLARE-MAR to start the agreed work before my 14-day cancellation period ends. I understand that if I cancel after work starts, I must pay a proportionate amount for work provided, and that I lose my cancellation right once the service is fully performed.” This request is optional; without it, work will normally wait until the cancellation period ends, unless a legal exception applies.

Where cancellation gives you a right to a refund, we will refund the amount due without undue delay and within 14 days after being informed of cancellation, using the original payment method unless you agree otherwise, without a refund fee. Any lawful proportionate charge for work supplied at your express request will be explained.
